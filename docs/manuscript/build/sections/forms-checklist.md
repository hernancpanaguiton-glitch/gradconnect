## Accomplished Forms Checklist

The following accomplished forms and materials must accompany the oral defense manuscript, as required by the Capstone Project Manual.

- **Appendix W: Capstone 42 Consultation Logs Form**, with all three prototype consultations signed by the adviser and Project Manager.
- **Appendix H: Censor/Technical Editor's Certificate**, signed after the censor reviews the oral defense manuscript.
- **Appendix AB: Capstone 42 Approval Sheet**, routed to all signatories after the oral defense.
- **Appendix AC: ACM-format manuscript**, prepared in LaTeX; five copies are submitted with the oral defense requirements.
- **Appendix AF: Capstone 42 CD/DVD**, with its contents prepared according to the contents guide and labeled according to Appendix AD.
