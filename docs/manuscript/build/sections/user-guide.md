### User Guide

This guide explains how each type of user works with GradConnect. Every user signs in at the system's address with their email and password. The left menu lists the pages available to that user's role; the bell at the top right shows notifications, and the profile menu beside it leads to Settings and Log Out.

![Sign-in page](storyboard-login.png)

#### 1. Graduate Student

**Completing the profile.** Open My Profile and work through its four tabs. On Basic Info, choose the college; the Program list then shows only that college's programs. Fill in the contact details and headline and click Save. The completion percentage at the top rises as the profile is filled in.

**Adding education and employment.** On the Education and Employment tabs, fill in the record and click Add. For a current job, tick "Currently working here", which removes the end date.

**Choosing skills.** On the Skills tab, skills are grouped by subject, and the group for the student's own college opens first. Type in the filter box to find a skill, click to select it, and click Save Skills.

![My Profile, Skills tab](guide-profile-skills.png)

**Managing résumés.** Open My Resumes to upload a résumé in PDF, Word (.docx) or text format; the first one uploaded becomes the primary résumé used for matching. Click "Build from My Profile" to create a formatted résumé from the profile. AI Resume Analysis compares the résumé with the profile and points out anything missing.

**Finding and applying for jobs.** Open Jobs & Internships, search or filter the list, and open a job to read the full advertisement. Click Apply to send an application. My Applications lists every application with its status, and an application can be withdrawn while it is still under consideration.

![Job details page with the Apply button](guide-job-details.png)

**Career tools.** Recommendations lists jobs matched to the student's résumé, best match first. Skill Gap Analysis compares the student's skills with those employers ask for and suggests learning resources. Career Progression shows the student's employment history over time. Career Readiness shows the student's readiness score after the readiness survey is answered, and Guidance Resources lists training and seminars.

**Surveys, concerns and messages.** Surveys lists the surveys open to the student; answer each question and submit. Student Concerns is used to file a concern with the Student Affairs Office, and Messages to contact a staff office.

#### 2. Alumni

Alumni use the same profile, résumé, job, recommendation, skill gap and career progression pages as graduate students, described above. They do not have Career Readiness, Guidance Resources or Student Concerns; instead, the Alumni Community lets alumni post updates and reply to one another. Tracer surveys from the Alumni Affairs Office appear under Surveys, and answers to questions linked to employment details, such as current employer or job title, update the alumnus's profile automatically.

![Alumni Community](guide-alumni-community.png)

#### 3. Industry Partner

**Company profile.** Open My Company to enter the company's name, industry, location and description, then click Save.

**Posting a job.** Open Job Postings and click New Posting. Enter the title, description, location and salary, and select the skills the job requires. While typing the title, the system suggests common job titles. Set the status to Open and save; the posting then appears on the job board and is matched against graduates' résumés.

![New Job Posting form](guide-new-job-posting.png)

**Reviewing applicants.** Open a posting's Candidates to see who applied, open each applicant's profile and résumé, and change the application status, for example to Shortlisted. The applicant is notified of each change. Matches lists the graduates whose résumés best fit the posting, ranked by score; click Refresh Matches to score them again.

![A posting’s Candidates page](guide-posting-candidates.png)
![A posting’s Matches page](guide-posting-matches.png)

**Finding talent.** Talent Search finds graduates by college, skill or name, and can be limited to graduates who have uploaded a résumé.

#### 4. Alumni Affairs Office

**Tracer surveys.** Open Tracer Survey and create a survey: give it a title, choose who it is for and when it opens and closes, and add questions. For a multiple-choice question, type the options separated by commas. Publishing the survey invites its respondents by notification and email. Results shows the answers to each question as charts or lists, and Remind sends a reminder to those who have not yet answered.

![Survey builder](guide-survey-builder.png)
![Survey results](guide-survey-results.png)

**Reports and records.** Employability Reports shows employment figures, which can be filtered by college and graduation year and exported. The Alumni Database lists graduates and can be searched.

**Engagement.** Announcements publishes news to a chosen group of users. Learning Resources adds training and seminars, which can be restricted to a program. Alumni Events manages events and attendance, the Alumni Community can be moderated, and Skill Taxonomy maintains the list of skills and their alternative names.

#### 5. Department Head

The Department Head's account belongs to one college, and every figure the account sees is limited to that college. The dashboard shows the number of graduates, the employment rate and placement by program. Employment Statistics and Program Outcomes break the results down by program, and each report can be exported for the college.

![Department Head dashboard](storyboard-department-head-dashboard.png)

#### 6. Student Affairs Office

Student Records lists students and can be searched. Career Readiness shows readiness results across all students. Scholarships records scholarship programs and their recipients, and Student Events manages events. Clearance & Records tracks each student's clearance office by office, and an office is marked as cleared once its requirements are met. Student Concerns lists the concerns students have filed; the office updates each concern's status and records how it was resolved.

![Clearance & Records](guide-clearance.png)

#### 7. Administrator

**Approving accounts.** Staff and employer accounts wait for approval after they register. Open User Management, open the pending account, set its status to Active and save; the user is notified that the account is approved. User Management is also used to change a user's roles or suspend an account.

![User Management](guide-user-management.png)

**Colleges and programs.** Colleges & Programs lists the university's colleges with their programs. New colleges and programs can be added and renamed. A college cannot be deleted while graduates or programs are still attached to it.

**Roles and system.** Roles & Permissions controls what each role may do; the built-in roles cannot be deleted. Job Management lists every posting on the platform and can close or remove any of them. Audit Logs records important actions, Platform Status shows the health of the background work and the AI services, System Settings holds platform-wide options such as the minimum match score shown to graduates, and Data & Backups creates and downloads database backups.
