GradConnect Testing Guide

A step-by-step walkthrough of every part of the system.

Work through it in order and tick each box as you go.

Your ticks are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

How to use this guide. Each check tells you what to do, then what should happen. If what you see matches, tick the box. If it does not, write it down — section 12 shows you exactly what to note.

You do not need to know how to code. You only need a web browser.

1Start the app

Do this once. It takes about five minutes the first time.

  1. Install Docker Desktop.

    Download it from docker.com, install, and open it. Wait until it says Engine running.

  2. Open a terminal in the project folder.

    In Windows File Explorer, open the gradconnect folder, type cmd in the address bar, and press Enter.

  3. Start the system.

    Type this and press Enter. The first run downloads a lot, so give it a few minutes.

    docker compose up --build
  4. Open the app.

    Go to http://localhost:8000 in your browser.

Open http://localhost:8000The GradConnect home page loads, with a blue banner and a "Get Started" button.
Open http://localhost:8025 in a second tabAn empty mail inbox called Mailpit. Every email the system sends will appear here — nothing is sent to real people.

Leave both tabs open for the whole testing session. Keep the terminal window open too — closing it stops the app.

To stop the app later, click the terminal and press Ctrl + C. To start again, run docker compose up.

The home page

Watch the blue "AI Job Match Found" card for about 15 secondsIt changes on its own, showing a different course each time — Computer Studies, Nursing, Criminology and so on.
Click one of the course names above the card (for example "Nursing")The card switches to that course and stops rotating on its own.
Click "Pause"The card stops changing. The button now says "Play".
Scroll to the bottom and click "Privacy Policy"A privacy page opens. The back arrow returns you to the home page.

3Create a new account

Anyone can sign up. Students and alumni get in straight away; every staff account has to be approved by an administrator first.

  1. From the home page, click "Get Started".
  2. Fill in the form and pick a role from the dropdown.
  3. Tick the privacy consent box, then click Register.
Open the "I am registering as" dropdownExactly seven choices: Graduate Student, Alumni, Industry Partner, Student Affairs Office, Alumni Affairs Office, Department Head, Admin.
Choose "Department Head"A College dropdown appears, listing all ten UCLM colleges.
Choose "Graduate Student" againThe College dropdown disappears. The notice says you will get a confirmation email.
Register as a Graduate Student with any made-up emailYou are signed in immediately and land on a dashboard.
Switch to the Mailpit tab (localhost:8025) and refreshA verification email has arrived. Open it and click the link inside.
Sign out. Register again, this time as "Admin"You are NOT signed in. A message says the account is waiting for administrator approval.
Try to sign in with that new Admin accountYou are refused. This is correct — nobody can approve themselves into an admin account.
Check Mailpit againAn email to the administrator says an account is awaiting approval. It names the role but NOT the person — personal details are deliberately kept out of email.

Remember this new account. You will approve it yourself in section 10, when you test the Administrator role.

4Graduate Student

Sign in as student@gradconnect.edu.ph. This is the biggest role, so take your time.

4.1 — Fill in your profile

  1. In the left menu, click "My Profile".
  2. Work through the four tabs across the top, one at a time.
Look at the College dropdown on the Basic Info tabAll ten UCLM colleges are listed — Computer Studies, Criminology, Nursing, Business and Accountancy, Customs Administration, Hospitality Management, Education, Marine Engineering, Marine Transportation, Engineering.
Pick "College of Nursing"The Program dropdown next to it now offers only Nursing programs.
Change the College to "College of Engineering"The Program resets — it will not keep a program from the wrong college.
Set College back to Computer Studies, fill in Phone, City and a Headline, then click SaveA green "saved" message. The "% complete" figure at the top goes up.
Type not-a-website into LinkedIn URL and saveA red message under that exact box tells you the link is not valid. Nothing is saved silently.

4.2 — Education and employment

On the Education tab, click Add with every box left emptyRed messages appear under Institution and Degree telling you they are required.
Now fill in Institution and Degree, then click AddThe record appears in the list above and the form clears.
On the Employment tab, tick "Currently working here"The End Date box greys out — you cannot set an end date for a job you still hold.
Untick it, set an End Date EARLIER than the Start Date, then click AddA red message says the end date must come after the start date.
Fix the dates and click AddThe job appears in the list.

4.3 — Skills

Open the Skills tabSkills are grouped in collapsible sections by subject — Computing & IT, Nursing & Healthcare, Soft Skills and so on. Your own course's group is already open.
Click several group headings to open and close themThey open and close smoothly. The page never goes blank.
Type "nurs" into the filter boxOnly matching skills remain, and their group is opened for you.
Clear the filter boxThe groups return to how you had them.
Click a few skills to select them, then click Save SkillsThey stay selected after the page saves.

4.4 — Résumé

Go to "My Resumes" and upload a PDF or Word (.docx) fileIt appears in the list. The first one you upload is marked Primary.
Try uploading a picture (.jpg or .png)It is refused with a clear message.
Click "Build from My Profile"A formatted résumé is built from what you typed in section 4.1. Check the dates read correctly — a job you have left must NOT say "Present".
Open "AI Resume Analysis"Either feedback on your résumé, or a clear message telling you to upload one first.

4.5 — Jobs and applications

Open "Jobs & Internships" and click any jobThe full advert opens. Check the salary reads sensibly, like ₱40,000–₱65,000 / month — never something like "₱40–000".
Click ApplyA confirmation appears and the button changes so you cannot apply twice.
Open "My Applications"Your application is listed with a status of Submitted.
Withdraw the applicationThe status changes to Withdrawn.
Use the search and filter boxes on the jobs pageThe list narrows. If there are many jobs, page links appear at the bottom and work.

4.6 — The rest of the menu

Open each of these and confirm the page loads with no blank screen and no error box.

RecommendationsSuggested jobs with a match score, or a message explaining none are ready yet.
Skill Gap AnalysisSkills you have against skills employers want.
Career ReadinessA readiness score, or an invitation to complete a survey. Any percentage shown must be between 0 and 100.
Career ProgressionYour employment history over time.
Guidance ResourcesTraining and seminar suggestions.
Surveys — open one and answer itYour answers save. Leaving a required question blank shows a message under that question.
Student Concerns — file a concernIt appears in your list of concerns.
Messages — start a conversation with a staff officeThe conversation opens and your message sends.
Notifications (the bell, top right)The panel opens on screen, fully visible. Clicking a notification opens it AND clears its unread dot.
Settings — turn a notification preference off, then reload the pageIt is still off. Your choice was saved.

5Alumni

Sign in as alumni@gradconnect.edu.ph. Very similar to a Graduate Student, with a community instead of student services.

Compare the left menu with the student'sYou now have "Alumni Community". You no longer have "Student Concerns".
Open "Alumni Community" and post an updateYour post appears at the top of the list.
Reply to a postYour comment appears underneath it.
Try posting a very long update (paste a page of text several times over)Either the box stops you typing past the limit, or a clear red message explains the problem. It must never just do nothing.
Run through Profile, Resumes, Job Board and ApplicationsThey behave exactly as they did in section 4.

6Industry Partner

Sign in as partner@gradconnect.edu.ph. This is the employer's view.

Open "My Company", change the Location, and click SaveA confirmation appears and the new value sticks after a reload.
Paste a very long Location (a few hundred characters) and saveA red message appears under that box. The Save button must never just flicker and do nothing.

6.1 — Post a job

  1. Click "Job Postings", then "New Posting".
  2. Fill in Title, Description, Location and Salary, and pick some skills.
  3. Set the status to Open and save.
Type slowly in the Job Title boxSuggestions appear. They always match what is currently in the box, never an older half-typed word.
Click several skill chips quickly, one after anotherEvery one you click stays selected. None un-select themselves.
Put a very long salary range in (over 100 characters) and saveA red message appears under the Salary box.
Fix it and save properlyThe posting appears in your list.
Sign in as the student in another window and apply to that jobBack as the partner, the applicant shows under "Candidates" for that posting.
Open Candidates for the postingEach applicant shows a link to their résumé. Their birthday, phone number and home address are NOT shown.
Move an applicant to ShortlistedThe status updates, and the applicant gets an email in Mailpit. The email must NOT contain their own name.
Open "Matches" for the posting and click "Refresh matches"The button stays disabled for a few seconds, then the list refreshes. Clicking twice quickly must not start two runs.
Look at the match score barsEvery bar sits between empty and full. A weak match must never show a completely full bar.
Open "Talent Search" and filter by collegeGraduates from that college are listed, including those on a specific program within it.

7Alumni Affairs Office

Sign in as alumni.affairs@gradconnect.edu.ph. This office runs tracer surveys and alumni engagement.

7.1 — Tracer surveys

Open "Tracer Survey"Each survey card shows a real number of questions — never the word "undefined".
Create a new survey with three questions, including one multiple-choiceFor the choice question you type the options separated by commas. It saves without complaint.
Set an "Opens at" date and time, save, then reopen the survey for editingThe date and time shown are the SAME ones you typed — not shifted by several hours.
Leave one question's text blank and saveA red message appears under that exact question.
Now delete a DIFFERENT question and save againThe red message stays on the right question. It must not jump to another one or vanish.
Publish the survey, answer it as the student, then come back and open ResultsYour answer is counted in the chart.
Open Results for a survey nobody has answeredIt says "No answers yet." — not a blank empty box.

Important check. Edit a survey that already has answers and change some wording, then save. Open Results again — the answers must still be there. Losing them would be a serious bug.

Edit an answered survey, then check ResultsAll previous answers are still counted.

7.2 — The rest of the menu

Alumni DatabaseA searchable list of graduates.
Employability ReportsCharts and figures. Type a graduation year in the filter — the report updates once you stop typing, not on every keystroke.
Reports HubLinks to each available report.
Announcements — publish one aimed at Graduate StudentsIt appears in the list. Students receive it; check Mailpit for the email.
Learning Resources — add one and restrict it to a programThe program dropdown groups programs under their college. The resource saves.
Skill Taxonomy — add an alias such as "JS" to JavaScriptThe alias is added to that skill.
Now try adding an alias that is already a skill's real name, such as "React"A neat red message appears next to the box. You must NOT get a full-page error screen.
Remove an alias by clicking its ×You are asked to confirm first.
Alumni Events, Alumni Community, Messages, SettingsEach page opens and works.

10Administrator

Sign in as admin@gradconnect.edu.ph. This account can see and change everything.

10.1 — Approve the account you made earlier

Open "User Management" and find the Admin account you registered in section 3It is listed with a status of Pending.
Edit it and set the status to ActiveIt saves. An approval email arrives in Mailpit.
Sign out and sign in with that accountYou now get in. Sign out again and return to the main admin account.

10.2 — Colleges and programs

Open "Colleges & Programs"All ten UCLM colleges, each with its degree programs underneath.
Add a new college — type the code in lower case, like ctestIt is saved in capitals (CTEST). Extra spaces in the name are tidied up.
Add a program under your new collegeIt appears nested under it.
Rename the college, then reload the pageThe new name is still there.
Try to delete "College of Computer Studies"You are refused, with a message explaining it still has graduates and programs attached.
Delete your test college and its program (delete the program first)You are asked to confirm, then both are removed.

10.3 — Safety rules

Edit your OWN admin account and try to set the status to SuspendedYou are refused. You cannot lock yourself out.
Try to remove the Admin role from yourselfYou are refused.
Edit any user and tick two roles quickly, one after the otherBoth stay ticked. Neither un-ticks itself.
Open "Roles & Permissions"The core roles cannot be deleted, and the Admin role's permissions cannot be edited away.

Data & BackupsThe page loads and lists any backups.
Tracer Surveys, Announcements, Reports, Reports Hub, Skill TaxonomyEach page opens.

11Test on a phone

Many students will use this on a phone, so this section matters. You can use a real phone on the same wi-fi, or your computer browser's phone preview.

  1. In Chrome or Edge, press F12.
  2. Click the small phone/tablet icon near the top left of the panel that opens.
  3. Choose a phone size such as iPhone SE or Pixel, then reload the page.

The one thing to watch for: you should never have to scroll sideways to read a page. Scrolling up and down is normal; scrolling left and right is a bug.

Home page — tap the ☰ menu buttonA menu opens with Features, For Employers, Contact, About, and Sign In / Get Started.
Home page — look at the AI Job Match cardIt is visible and readable on the phone, and the course chips scroll sideways within their own row.
Sign in, then tap the ☰ button at the top leftThe menu slides in. Tapping outside it, or pressing Escape, closes it. The page behind must not scroll while it is open.
Tap the magnifying glass in the top barA search box opens across the screen. Closing it clears what you typed.
Tap the bell iconThe notifications panel is fully on screen — not cut off at the edge.
Open "My Profile" and swipe across the four tabsThe tab strip scrolls sideways by itself. The page underneath does not.
Check the form boxes on the profile pageThey are stacked one per line and full width. No text is cut off.
Open Messages, then open a conversationThe conversation fills the screen and has a back arrow to return to the list.
As an admin, open User ManagementThe table scrolls sideways inside its own box, so you can still reach the Edit and Delete buttons.
Visit five or six more pages at phone sizeNone of them scroll sideways.

12Report a problem

A good report can be acted on straight away. A vague one cannot.

For anything that does not match this guide, write down these five things:

Write downExample
1. Who you wereSigned in as partner@gradconnect.edu.ph
2. Where you wereJob Postings → New Posting
3. What you didTyped a 200-character salary and clicked Publish
4. What you expectedA red message under the Salary box
5. What happened insteadNothing at all — the button flickered and the form stayed

Always add a screenshot. On Windows press Windows + Shift + S. Include the whole browser window, with the web address visible.

If the screen goes completely white

This matters most of all, so capture it properly:

  1. Press F12 to open the developer panel.
  2. Click the "Console" tab.
  3. Screenshot any red text you see there.
  4. Note the exact web address and what you clicked immediately before.

13Known gaps (not bugs)

These are expected. Please do not report them.

What you will seeWhy
AI features return nothing, or a "not configured" noteThe AI service needs a paid key. Without one, the system deliberately falls back instead of failing.
No real emails arriveBy design. Everything goes to Mailpit at localhost:8025.
Emails never mention anybody's nameDeliberate. Personal details stay inside the app, behind the sign-in.
Some pages look plain in dark modeDark mode is not finished on every page yet.
Home page figures like "4,820+ Graduates Placed"Sample marketing text, not live data.
Match scores are missing on brand-new postingsMatching runs in the background. Wait a moment and reload.
