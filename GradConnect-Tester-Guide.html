GradConnect Testing Guide

A step-by-step walkthrough of every part of the system, on the live test site gradconnect.tech.

Work through it in order and tick each box as you go.

Your ticks are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

How to use this guide. Each check tells you what to do, then what should happen. If what you see matches, tick the box. If it does not, write it down — section 15 shows you exactly what to note.

You do not need to install anything or know how to code. You only need a web browser and, for section 12, a phone.

The order matters. The test site starts empty, so earlier sections create what later ones use. The partner posts a job before the student applies to it, and the Alumni Affairs Office publishes a survey before the student answers it. Please do the sections in order.

1Before you start

Everything happens on two web addresses. Nothing needs installing.

WhatAddressSign-in
The apphttps://gradconnect.techThe test accounts in section 2
The test inboxhttps://inbox.gradconnect.techFrom your project lead

About the test inbox. This is a test site, so it never emails real people. Every email it sends — confirmations, approvals, notifications — lands in this one inbox instead. Keep it open in a second tab for the whole session.

Passwords come from your project lead, never from this guide. Do not share them outside the testing team.

You are sharing this site with other testers.

• Start the name of everything you create with your initials, for example a job called [EL] Junior Developer. Then you can find your own items, and so can whoever reads your report.

• Only edit or delete things you created yourself.

• Never change the password, email or role of the shared test accounts in section 2. Everyone else would be locked out.

3Create a new account

Anyone can sign up. Students and alumni confirm their email and get straight in; every other role waits for an administrator. You can use a made-up email address — the email goes to the test inbox, not to a real mailbox.

  1. Sign out. From the home page, click "Get Started".
  2. Fill in the form and pick a role from the dropdown.
  3. Tick the privacy consent box, then click Register.
Open the "I am registering as" dropdownExactly seven choices: Graduate Student, Alumni, Industry Partner, Student Affairs Office, Alumni Affairs Office, Department Head, Admin.
Choose "Department Head"A College dropdown appears, listing all ten UCLM colleges.
Choose "Graduate Student" againThe College dropdown disappears. The notice says you will get a confirmation email.
Register as a Graduate Student with a made-up email, such as el.test1@example.comYou are signed in, and asked to confirm your email address.
Switch to the test inbox tab and refreshA verification email to that address has arrived. Open it and click the link — you land on your new dashboard.
Sign out. Register again with another made-up email, this time as "Admin"You are NOT signed in. A message says the account is waiting for administrator approval.
Try to sign in with that new Admin accountYou are refused. This is correct — nobody can approve themselves into an admin account.
Check the test inbox againAn email to the administrators says an account is awaiting approval. It names the role but NOT the person — personal details are deliberately kept out of email.

Remember that new Admin account's email. You approve it yourself in the next section.

4Administrator

Sign in as admin@gradconnect.tech. This account can see and change everything, so be careful to touch only your own test items.

4.1 — Approve the account you made in section 3

Open "User Management" and find the Admin account you registeredIt is listed with a status of Pending.
Edit it and set the status to ActiveIt saves. An approval email appears in the test inbox.
Sign out and sign in with that accountYou now get in. Sign out again and return to admin@gradconnect.tech.

4.2 — Colleges and programs

Open "Colleges & Programs"All ten UCLM colleges, each with its degree programs underneath.
Add a test college whose code is your initials in lower case, like xelIt is saved in capitals (XEL). Extra spaces in the name are tidied up.
Add a program under your test collegeIt appears nested under it.
Rename your test college, then reload the pageThe new name is still there.
Try to delete "College of Computer Studies"You are refused, with a message explaining what is still attached to it.
Delete your test college and its program (delete the program first)You are asked to confirm, then both are removed.

4.3 — Safety rules

Edit your OWN account and try to set the status to SuspendedYou are refused. You cannot lock yourself out.
Try to remove the Admin role from yourselfYou are refused.
Edit the Graduate Student account YOU registered in section 3 and tick two roles quickly, one after the otherBoth stay ticked. Neither un-ticks itself. Untick them again and save, so the account is back to Graduate Student. (Never do this to the shared accounts.)
Open "Roles & Permissions"The core roles cannot be deleted, and the Admin role's permissions cannot be edited away.

4.4 — The rest of the menu

Audit LogsA record of what has been done — including the college you added and deleted just now.
Platform StatusSystem health figures.
System Settings — clear the "minimum fit score" box completely and saveA red message asks for a number. It must NOT quietly save as 0. Do not save any other change here: it affects every tester.
Data & BackupsThe page loads. Do not restore anything: a restore replaces everyone's test data.
Tracer Surveys, Announcements, Reports, Reports Hub, Skill TaxonomyEach page opens.

Job Management is checked in section 11, once there are jobs to see.

5Industry Partner — set up and post a job

Sign in as partner@gradconnect.tech. This is the employer's view. The job you post here is the one the student applies to in section 7.

5.1 — Company profile

Open "My Company"Either an empty form to create the company profile, or the profile another tester already created. If it exists, skip the next check.
If empty: fill in the company name, industry and location, then SaveA "Company created" message. The details stay after a reload.
Paste a very long Location (a few hundred characters) and saveA red message appears under that box. The Save button must never just flicker and do nothing. Put the location back afterwards.

5.2 — Post a job

  1. Click "Job Postings", then "New Posting".
  2. Fill in Title (starting with your initials), Description, Location and Salary, and pick some skills — include PHP and Laravel.
  3. Set the status to Open and save.
Type slowly in the Job Title boxSuggestions appear. They always match what is currently in the box, never an older half-typed word.
Click several skill chips quickly, one after anotherEvery one you click stays selected. None un-select themselves.
Click "Generate from title" for the descriptionThe AI writes a job description based on your title. You can edit it before saving.
Put a very long salary range in (over 100 characters) and saveA red message appears under the Salary box.
Fix it, for example 40000-65000, and save properlyThe posting appears in your list.
Open "Talent Search" and filter by collegeGraduates from that college are listed (there may be only a few on the test site).

6Alumni Affairs Office

Sign in as alumni.affairs@gradconnect.tech. This office runs tracer surveys and alumni engagement.

6.1 — Tracer surveys

Open "Tracer Survey"Each survey card shows a real number of questions — never the word "undefined".
Create a survey (title starting with your initials), aimed at Graduate Students, with three questions including one multiple-choiceFor the choice question you type the options separated by commas. It saves without complaint.
Set an "Opens at" date and time, save, then reopen the survey for editingThe date and time shown are the SAME ones you typed — not shifted by several hours.
Leave one question's text blank and saveA red message appears under that exact question.
Now delete a DIFFERENT question and save againThe red message stays on the right question. It must not jump to another one or vanish.
Fix the blank question, make sure the survey opens now (not in the future), and publish itIt is published. The student answers it in section 7.
Open Results for your surveyIt says "No answers yet." — not a blank empty box.

6.2 — The rest of the menu

Announcements — publish one aimed at Graduate Students (title with your initials)It appears in the list, and an email appears in the test inbox.
Learning Resources — add one and restrict it to a programThe program dropdown groups programs under their college. The resource saves.
Skill Taxonomy — add an alias such as "JS" to JavaScriptThe alias is added to that skill.
Now try adding an alias that is already a skill's real name, such as "React"A neat red message appears next to the box. You must NOT get a full-page error screen.
Remove your alias by clicking its ×You are asked to confirm first.
Alumni Database, Employability Reports, Reports HubEach opens. In Employability Reports, type a graduation year in the filter — the report updates once you stop typing, not on every keystroke. Figures are small: the test site starts empty.
Alumni Events, Alumni Community, Messages, SettingsEach page opens and works.

7Graduate Student

Sign in as student@gradconnect.tech. This is the biggest role, so take your time.

7.1 — Fill in your profile

  1. In the left menu, click "My Profile".
  2. Work through the four tabs across the top, one at a time.
Look at the College dropdown on the Basic Info tabAll ten UCLM colleges are listed — Computer Studies, Criminology, Nursing, Business and Accountancy, Customs Administration, Hospitality Management, Education, Marine Engineering, Marine Transportation, Engineering.
Pick "College of Nursing"The Program dropdown next to it now offers only Nursing programs.
Change the College to "College of Engineering"The Program resets — it will not keep a program from the wrong college.
Set College to Computer Studies and pick a program, fill in Phone, City and a Headline, then click SaveA green "saved" message. The "% complete" figure at the top goes up.
Type not-a-website into LinkedIn URL and saveA red message under that exact box tells you the link is not valid. Nothing is saved silently.

7.2 — Education and employment

On the Education tab, click Add with every box left emptyRed messages appear under Institution and Degree telling you they are required.
Now fill in Institution and Degree, then click AddThe record appears in the list above and the form clears.
On the Employment tab, tick "Currently working here"The End Date box greys out — you cannot set an end date for a job you still hold.
Untick it, set an End Date EARLIER than the Start Date, then click AddA red message says the end date must come after the start date.
Fix the dates and click AddThe job appears in the list.

7.3 — Skills

Open the Skills tabSkills are grouped in collapsible sections by subject — Computing & IT, Nursing & Healthcare, Soft Skills and so on. Your own college's group is already open.
Click several group headings to open and close themThey open and close smoothly. The page never goes blank.
Type "nurs" into the filter boxOnly matching skills remain, and their group is opened for you.
Clear the filter boxThe groups return to how you had them.
Select PHP, Laravel and a few others, then click Save SkillsThey stay selected after the page saves.

7.4 — Résumé and AI

Go to "My Resumes" and upload a PDF or Word (.docx) résumé (up to 10 MB)It appears in the list. The first one you upload is marked Primary.
Try uploading a picture (.jpg or .png)It is refused with a clear message.
Click "Build from My Profile"A formatted résumé is built from what you typed in 7.1–7.3. Check the dates read correctly — a job you have left must NOT say "Present".
Open "AI Resume Analysis"AI feedback on your uploaded résumé, or a clear message telling you what is missing.
Wait a minute, then open "Recommendations"Jobs matched to your résumé, each with a fit score, a short explanation, matched skills and missing skills. With an IT résumé, the partner's job from section 5 should be among them. If none show yet, wait another minute and reload.
Mark one recommendation helpful or not helpfulYour choice is saved.
Open "Skill Gap Analysis"The skills you have against the skills the jobs ask for, with what to learn next.

7.5 — Jobs and applications

Open "Jobs & Internships" and find the partner's job (it starts with the initials used in section 5)The full advert opens. The salary reads sensibly, like ₱40,000–₱65,000 / month — never something like "₱40–000".
Click ApplyA confirmation appears and the button changes so you cannot apply twice.
Open "My Applications"Your application is listed with a status of Submitted. Leave it there — the partner reviews it in section 11.
Use the search and filter boxes on the jobs pageThe list narrows as you type and filter.

7.6 — The rest of the menu

Surveys — open the survey from section 6 and answer itYour answers save. Leaving a required question blank shows a message under that question.
Student Concerns — file a concern (subject starting with your initials)It appears in your list of concerns. The Student Affairs Office replies in section 9.
Career ReadinessA readiness score, or an invitation to complete an assessment. Any percentage shown is between 0 and 100.
Career Progression and Guidance ResourcesYour employment history over time, and training suggestions — including the learning resource from section 6 if it matches your program.
Messages — start a conversation with a staff officeThe conversation opens and your message sends.
Notifications (the bell, top right)The panel opens fully on screen. The announcement from section 6 is there. Clicking a notification opens it AND clears its unread dot.
Settings — turn a notification preference off, then reload the pageIt is still off. Turn it back on afterwards.

11Bringing it together

Now the pieces meet: the partner reviews the student's application, and the survey office sees the student's answers. A private window lets you be both accounts at once.

11.1 — The partner reviews the applicant

Sign in as partner@gradconnect.tech and open your job from section 5.

Open Candidates for the postingThe test student is listed, with a link to their résumé. Their birthday, phone number and home address are NOT shown.
Move the applicant to ShortlistedThe status updates, and an email to the applicant appears in the test inbox. The email must NOT contain the applicant's name.
Open "Matches" for the posting and click "Refresh matches"The button stays disabled for a few seconds, then the list refreshes with the test student and a fit score. Clicking twice quickly must not start two runs.
Look at the match score barsEvery bar sits between empty and full. A weak match never shows a completely full bar.

11.2 — The student sees the outcome

Sign in as student@gradconnect.tech.

Open "My Applications"The application now says Shortlisted.
Withdraw the applicationThe status changes to Withdrawn.

11.3 — The survey office sees the answers

Sign in as alumni.affairs@gradconnect.tech and open your survey from section 6.

Open ResultsThe student's answer is counted in the chart.

Important check. Edit that survey, change some wording, and save. Open Results again — the answers must still be there. Losing them would be a serious bug.

Edit the answered survey, then check ResultsAll previous answers are still counted.

11.4 — The administrator sees it all

Sign in as admin@gradconnect.tech.

Job ManagementEvery posting on the site, including the partner's job. If there are more than 20, page links appear at the bottom and work.
Audit LogsToday's actions are recorded, such as the account you approved.

12Test on a phone

Many students will use this on a phone, so this section matters. The site is on the internet, so use your own phone — mobile data or any wi-fi.

The one thing to watch for: you should never have to scroll sideways to read a page. Scrolling up and down is normal; scrolling left and right is a bug.

Open gradconnect.tech on your phone and tap the ☰ menu buttonA menu opens with Features, For Employers, Contact, About, and Sign In / Get Started.
Home page — look at the AI Job Match cardIt is visible and readable, and the course chips scroll sideways within their own row.
Sign in as the student, then tap the ☰ button at the top leftThe menu slides in. Tapping outside it closes it. The page behind must not scroll while it is open.
Tap the magnifying glass in the top barA search box opens across the screen. Closing it clears what you typed.
Tap the bell iconThe notifications panel is fully on screen — not cut off at the edge.
Open "My Profile" and swipe across the four tabsThe tab strip scrolls sideways by itself. The page underneath does not.
Check the form boxes on the profile pageThey are stacked one per line and full width. No text is cut off.
Upload a résumé from your phone (from Files or Google Drive)It uploads and appears in My Resumes.
Open Messages, then open a conversationThe conversation fills the screen and has a back arrow to return to the list.
Sign in as the admin and open User ManagementThe table scrolls sideways inside its own box, so you can still reach the Edit and Delete buttons.
Visit five or six more pages on the phoneNone of them scroll sideways.

13Light and dark mode

The app has two colour themes. Both must be readable. The switch is the moon/sun icon in the top bar once you are signed in.

What you are checking: that no text ever disappears into its background. If you can see a heading, a label, or what you typed, it passes.

Sign in, then click the moon icon in the top barThe whole app turns dark. Every heading, label and paragraph is still easy to read.
Open "My Profile" and type into a boxWhat you type is clearly visible — light text on a dark box. It must never be invisible.
Look at the faint example text inside empty boxes (such as "Cebu City")Readable grey — dimmer than real text, but not close to invisible.
Open a dropdown, for example CollegeThe list of choices is readable, not dark text on a dark strip.
Visit five more pages while still in dark modeNo page has text that vanishes into the background.
Still in dark mode, open Résumé Builder and press Ctrl + PThe print preview is black text on white paper — not a dark page, and never blank.
Click the sun icon to switch backEverything returns to the light theme and is still readable.
Sign out and look at the home page in both themesThe headings ("Everything You Need to Succeed" and the card titles) are readable in both.

14Automated tests (team members)

For team members who have the project and Docker Desktop on their own computer. If you only test through the browser, skip this section. About 640 automated tests check the system by themselves in a few minutes. They run on your computer, never on the live site, and cannot change its data.

  1. Open a terminal in the project folder.

    In Windows File Explorer, open the gradconnect folder, type cmd in the address bar, and press Enter.

  2. Run the back-end tests.

    These check the pages, the permissions of every role, forms, emails and reports. The first run builds for a few minutes.

    docker compose run --rm --build php-tests
  3. Run the front-end tests.

    These check the screens themselves: forms, buttons, menus, charts. The first run downloads packages for a few minutes.

    docker compose run --rm frontend-tests
Read the last lines of the back-end runTests: 5 skipped, 489 passed (or more passed, if tests were added since) — and the word “failed” appears nowhere.
Look at the 5 skipped testsEach one says it Requires PostgreSQL + pgvector. That is expected: they test the AI vector search, which the temporary test database cannot do.
Read the last lines of the front-end runTest Files 28 passed and Tests 151 passed, with no “failed”.

15Report a problem

A good report can be acted on straight away. A vague one cannot.

For anything that does not match this guide, write down these five things:

Write downExample
1. Who you wereSigned in as partner@gradconnect.tech, on a phone
2. Where you wereJob Postings → New Posting
3. What you didTyped a 200-character salary and clicked Publish
4. What you expectedA red message under the Salary box
5. What happened insteadNothing at all — the button flickered and the form stayed

Always add a screenshot. On Windows press Windows + Shift + S. Include the whole browser window, with the web address visible. Add the date and time, so the team can find it in the server's logs.

If the screen goes completely white, or says "Server Error"

This matters most of all, so capture it properly:

  1. On a computer, press F12 to open the developer panel.
  2. Click the "Console" tab.
  3. Screenshot any red text you see there.
  4. Note the exact web address, the time, and what you clicked immediately before.

16Known gaps (not bugs)

These are expected. Please do not report them.

What you will seeWhy
No email reaches a real mailboxBy design on the test site. Everything goes to the test inbox at inbox.gradconnect.tech.
Emails never mention anybody's nameDeliberate. Personal details stay inside the app, behind the sign-in.
Recommendations and match scores appear a minute or two after a résumé upload or a new jobThe AI works in the background, so pages stay fast. Wait a moment and reload.
Reports and dashboards show small numbersThe test site started empty; it only holds what testers created.
Jobs, posts and surveys you did not createOther testers share the site. Leave them alone.
Home page figures like "4,820+ Graduates Placed"Sample marketing text, not live data.
